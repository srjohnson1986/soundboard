# Code of conduct

Soundboard exists to help people communicate, and the people who use it, and the people
who build it, deserve to be treated with respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and say so when you disagree.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Respect that many people here are non-experts, including people who rely on the app
  to speak and the families who set it up for them. Plain language is welcome.
- Don't share anyone's private information, including recordings and board contents,
  without their permission.

## What we won't accept

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, threats, and publishing
other people's private information. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other space for the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, use a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/srjohnson1986/soundboard/security/advisories/new)
on the repository (it reaches the maintainer and nobody else), or contact the maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people
who break these rules.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
